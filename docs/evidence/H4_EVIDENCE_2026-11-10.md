## Accidental rollback (fail-closed evidence)
At 18:04:57Z the operator ran the runbook's rollback block by mistake. It removed `zzz-h4.conf`, reloaded systemd and restarted the service, so the unit fell back to `zz-r5.conf` (the R5 binary `692992d`).

That binary **refused to start**: `named principal and future token expiry within 24 hours required`, exit 1, unit `failed`. This is the expected fail-closed behavior of the old writer against a 7-day token: it never runs with a lifetime it does not support.

Recovery: `zzz-h4.conf` was restored and the service restarted. H4 came up at 18:07:51Z. Nothing was lost: no executions were in flight.
